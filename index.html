About Me

I am a physics graduate from the University of Dhaka with research experience in computational condensed matter physics, DFT-based materials simulation, liquid-metal surface modeling, nonlinear dynamics, molecular dynamics, and soft-matter self-propulsion.

My work connects microscopic interactions, electronic structure, transport processes, and nonlinear behavior with measurable physical properties. I am especially interested in graduate research combining computational modeling with experimentally relevant questions in condensed matter physics and materials science.

Research Projects

01

COMPUTATIONAL CONDENSED MATTER

Surface Tension of 3d Liquid Metals

Investigated the microscopic origin of surface tension in liquid transition metals using pseudopotential-based numerical modeling and liquid-state structural information.

  • Bretonnet–Silbert pseudopotential approach
  • Structure factors and local-field corrections
  • Comparison with literature and experimental trends
Presented at the National Conference on Physics 2025
02

FIRST-PRINCIPLES MATERIALS PHYSICS

Lead-Free Double Perovskites

First-principles study of structural, electronic, optical, mechanical, and thermoelectric behavior in environmentally safer double-perovskite materials.

  • VASP and Quantum ESPRESSO workflows
  • Relaxation and convergence testing
  • Band structure, DOS, optical and mechanical analysis
Ongoing research
03

SOFT MATTER & MOLECULAR SIMULATION

Source–Sink Chemistry and Marangoni Propulsion

Studying how concentration gradients, transport, and surface-tension differences can produce directed motion in self-propelled droplet systems.

  • LAMMPS-based molecular simulation
  • Diffusion, viscosity and concentration analysis
  • Source–sink chemistry and motion stability
Ongoing research
04

NONLINEAR DYNAMICS

Variable-Dependent Quantification of Chaos

Examined stability, sensitivity to initial conditions, phase-space structure, and variable-dependent measures of complexity in nonlinear systems.

  • Numerical integration and parameter sweeps
  • Chaotic attractors and phase portraits
  • Entropy and autocorrelation analysis
