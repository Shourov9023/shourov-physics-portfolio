AL AMIN WAZED SHOUROV

PHYSICIST, RESEARCHER, TRAVELER, SOCCER FAN

I am a physics graduate from the University of Dhaka, Bangladesh with research experience in computational condensed matter physics, density functional theory, liquid-metal surface modeling, nonlinear dynamics, molecular dynamics, and soft matter physics.

My research interests center on understanding how microscopic interactions, electronic structure, transport processes, and nonlinear behavior give rise to measurable physical properties. I am particularly interested in problems where numerical modeling can be connected with experimentally relevant questions in condensed matter physics and materials science.

My current and recent work includes first-principles modeling of lead-free double perovskites, numerical investigation of surface tension in liquid transition metals, molecular simulation of Marangoni-driven self-propulsion, and computational analysis of nonlinear chaotic systems.

RESEARCH EXPERIENCE

COMPUTATIONAL CONDENSED MATTER PHYSICS

Surface Tension of 3d Liquid Metals: A Role of the Structure-Dependent Part

Condensed Matter Physics Research Group, Department of Physics, University of Dhaka

Supervisor: Dr. Ratan Chandra Gosh · Mar 2024–Jun 2025

Studied the microscopic origin of surface tension in liquid transition metals using pseudopotential-based numerical modeling and liquid state structural information.

  • Implemented the Bretonnet–Silbert pseudopotential approach.
  • Examined the influence of model potentials, structure factors, and local field correction functions.
  • Compared calculated surface behavior with published literature and experimental trends.
  • Investigated why transition-metal bonding makes simple-liquid modeling challenging.

Presented at the National Conference on Physics 2025, organized by the Bangladesh Physical Society.

Currently under review in the Applied Surface Science Journal.

FIRST-PRINCIPLES MATERIALS PHYSICS

First-Principles Design of Pb-Free Double Perovskites for Optoelectronic, Mechanical, and Thermoelectric Applications

Computational Physics Research Group, Department of Physics, University of Dhaka

Supervisor: Dr. Alamgir Kabir · Apr 2026–Present

Investigating lead-free double-perovskite materials through first-principles calculations to understand their structural, electronic, optical, mechanical, vibrational, and thermoelectric behavior.

  • Designed DFT workflows using VASP and Quantum ESPRESSO.
  • Performed structure preparation, relaxation, and convergence testing.
  • Studied band structures, density of states, charge behavior, phonon stability, and optical response.
  • Evaluated mechanical stability and thermoelectric indicators.

Ongoing research project.

SOFT MATTER AND MOLECULAR SIMULATION

Molecular Dynamics Simulation of Decanol and Organic Liquids for Marangoni propulsion

Condensed Matter Physics Research Group, Department of Physics, University of Dhaka

Supervisor: Dr. Ratan Chandra Gosh · Apr 2026–Present

Studying how source–sink chemistry, molecular transport, concentration gradients, and surface-tension differences can generate directed motion in active droplet systems.

  • Developing LAMMPS-based molecular simulation workflows.
  • Examining diffusion, contact time ,viscosity, and concentration profiles.
  • Investigating the physical origin and stability of Marangoni-driven motion.
  • Connecting microscopic molecular behavior with measurable soft-matter response.

Ongoing research project.
