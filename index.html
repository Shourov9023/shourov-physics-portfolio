AL AMIN WAZED SHOUROV

PHYSICIST, RESEARCHER, COMPUTATIONAL MODELER

I am a physics graduate from the University of Dhaka with research experience in computational condensed matter physics, density functional theory, liquid-metal surface modeling, nonlinear dynamics, molecular dynamics, and soft matter physics.

My work focuses on understanding how microscopic interactions, electronic structure, transport processes, and nonlinear behavior determine measurable physical properties. I am especially interested in combining numerical modeling with experimentally relevant problems in condensed matter physics and materials science.

My current work includes first-principles investigation of lead-free double perovskites, numerical modeling of surface tension in liquid transition metals, and molecular simulation of Marangoni-driven self-propulsion.

Condensed Matter Physics Research Group, University of Dhaka · Mar 2024–Jun 2025
